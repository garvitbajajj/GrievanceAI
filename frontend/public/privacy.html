Privacy Policy

GrievanceAI · Last updated 25 September 2026

GrievanceAI is a student portfolio project that helps citizens describe grievances in Indian languages and find the right government portal. It is not a government service, and filing here does not submit a complaint to any authority.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email and profile picture URL.
  • Grievance content: the text you type, voice recordings, and images or PDFs you attach.
  • Contact and location details you enter: name, phone number, state, district, pincode, address and landmark.
  • Settings: preferred language and, if you enable it, a two-factor authentication factor.

How we use it

  • Your grievance text, recordings and attachments are sent to Google's Gemini API to transcribe, translate, summarise and categorise the complaint, and to suggest nearby offices.
  • Your email is used to sign you in and, if enabled, to send status and follow-up emails about your grievances.
  • Confirmed grievance text and categories may be kept to evaluate and improve classification accuracy.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Attachments are kept in a private bucket and are only served through short-lived signed links. The website is hosted on Vercel. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

You can see your own grievances. Accounts with an admin or authority role can view and update all grievances in order to process them.

Deleting your data

You can discard a grievance before submitting it. To delete your account and all associated data, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository and it will be removed.
